# Design direction

Based on the five supplied screenshots of The Agile Monkeys application.

Palette: ink #080808, paper #ffffff, banana #ffdf24, soft grey #f4f4f2, secondary text #60605a, rule #deded8.
Type: locally bundled DM Sans, bold geometric display and readable regular text. No remote font requests.
Layout: restrained masthead, horizontal five-stop vine, left-aligned questions and application responses. Intro pairs a large headline with an original SVG monkey hanging in a yellow illustration panel. Portrait video retains its native aspect ratio.

The memorable gesture is the monkey: a single authored SVG character in the hero and a smaller version that swings to the next step after navigation. No ambient looping animations.

Review against brief: use the supplied black/white/yellow identity, not an unrelated jungle aesthetic. At Sascha’s request, lightly tidy spelling, grammar and paragraph breaks while preserving his meaning and rough-and-ready voice. Clearly separate summary text and site-building and copy-editing AI disclosure from original responses. Avoid implying official affiliation or a successful form submission. Bundle React as a classic script so file:// opening needs no server.
